Overview

International trade depends on efficient clearance.

Stratex Logistics provides professional customs clearance services designed to simplify complex import and export procedures and facilitate the timely movement of cargo.

Our customs operations combine documentation management, regulatory coordination and port-level execution to provide customers with greater visibility and control throughout the clearance process.

Services

Customs services

End-to-end clearance support for import and export shipments.

  • Import customs clearance

    Clearance of inbound shipments, from document review to the release of cargo from the port or terminal.

  • Export customs clearance

    Preparation and filing for outbound shipments so cargo is cleared for loading on time.

  • Customs documentation

    Preparation and checking of clearance documents such as bills of entry, shipping bills, invoices and packing lists.

  • Regulatory coordination

    Coordination with the authorities and agencies involved in clearing your cargo.

  • Port and terminal coordination

    On-the-ground coordination with ports, terminals and container freight stations.

  • Cargo clearance management

    Following each shipment through the clearance process and keeping you informed at every stage.

  • Import and export compliance support

    Guidance on documentation and procedural requirements to help avoid delays and unnecessary costs.

How it works

A clear path through clearance.

We manage each stage so your cargo keeps moving.

  1. Documents received

    You share your shipment documents and cargo details with our team.

  2. Review

    Documents are checked for accuracy and completeness before anything is filed.

  3. Filing

    Declarations are prepared and filed for your import or export shipment.

  4. Coordination

    We coordinate with authorities, ports and terminals through assessment and examination.

  5. Release

    Cleared cargo is released and handed over for storage, transport or onward delivery.
