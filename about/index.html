Who we are

We are developing a network of purpose-built logistics infrastructure across key trade and port-connected locations in India, enabling businesses to store, clear and move critical cargo with greater efficiency and control.

Our approach combines strategic infrastructure, operational precision and logistics expertise to create dependable solutions for modern supply chains.

Our philosophy

Guided by three principles.

We believe the future of logistics will be defined not only by the movement of goods, but by the quality of the infrastructure supporting that movement.

Our investments are therefore guided by three principles.

Strategic locations

Infrastructure positioned around major trade, industrial and maritime corridors.

Operational excellence

Processes designed around consistency, accountability and precision.

Long-term scale

A growing network of facilities and capabilities built to support the changing requirements of modern supply chains.

Our vision

Building the infrastructure behind tomorrow’s supply chains.

India’s logistics ecosystem is undergoing a fundamental transformation. As trade expands and supply chains become increasingly specialised, the demand for reliable cold-chain infrastructure and integrated logistics services continues to grow.

Stratex Logistics is positioning itself at the intersection of this transformation. Through strategic infrastructure investments and integrated logistics capabilities, we are building a platform designed to connect ports, businesses, products and markets more efficiently.

Our ambition is not simply to move cargo. It is to build the infrastructure that enables businesses to move forward.
