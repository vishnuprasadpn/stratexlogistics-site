Overview

Preserving quality. Protecting value.

Cold-chain logistics is not simply about maintaining temperature. It is about protecting product integrity throughout the supply chain.

Stratex Logistics is building a growing portfolio of temperature-controlled warehousing infrastructure designed to support businesses handling products where consistency, security and controlled conditions are essential.

Our facilities are being developed at strategically significant locations, connecting storage infrastructure with major trade and logistics corridors.

Each facility is being planned around one idea: the conditions your product needs should be maintained from the moment cargo arrives until the moment it leaves.

Capabilities

Cold storage capabilities

Everything needed to keep temperature-sensitive cargo in the right conditions, and to move it on when it is needed.

  • Temperature-controlled storage

    Purpose-built environments designed for temperature-sensitive cargo, with conditions planned around the needs of each product.

  • Secure warehousing

    Controlled facilities designed around product protection and operational reliability, with managed access and organised inventory.

  • Cargo handling

    Structured inbound and outbound processes for efficient movement of goods, keeping time outside controlled conditions to a minimum.

  • Integrated logistics

    Seamless coordination between warehousing, customs, transportation and distribution, through a single point of contact.

  • Scalable infrastructure

    A growing network designed to support evolving customer requirements, from regional storage to port-connected capacity.

Who it is for

Built for products where conditions matter.

Our cold-chain infrastructure is being designed for businesses that cannot afford to compromise on how their products are stored.

  • Seafood and marine products

    Products that depend on consistent low temperatures from landing or import through to distribution.

  • Fruit and vegetables

    Fresh produce that needs controlled conditions to protect quality and shelf life.

  • Dairy and frozen foods

    Chilled and frozen goods that must stay within their required temperature range.

  • Processed and packaged foods

    Finished food products held ahead of onward distribution or export.

  • Import and export cargo

    Temperature-sensitive shipments that need storage alongside customs clearance and port movement.

  • Other sensitive goods

    Any product with specific storage requirements. Tell us what you need and we will assess suitability.

How it works

From arrival to dispatch.

A structured process designed to keep your cargo in the right conditions at every stage.

  1. Requirement assessment

    We understand your product, volumes, temperature range and timelines.

  2. Storage planning

    Space, handling and conditions are planned around your cargo before it arrives.

  3. Receiving

    Inbound cargo is checked, recorded and moved promptly into controlled storage.

  4. Controlled storage

    Your product is held in the right conditions with organised inventory.

  5. Dispatch

    Outbound cargo is prepared and coordinated with transport, customs or onward delivery.
